# Platform Notes — last verified July 2026

⚠️ Always re-search before using these — thresholds shift. This is a fallback/starting reference only.

## YouTube Partner Program (YPP) — as of mid-2026
Two tiers now exist:
- **Tier 1 (early access / fan funding):** 500 subscribers + 3 public videos in last 90 days + either 3,000 watch hours (12 months) OR 3M Shorts views (90 days). Unlocks Super Thanks, Memberships, Shopping tagging early.
- **Tier 2 (full ad revenue):** 1,000 subscribers + either 4,000 valid public watch hours (12 months) OR 10M Shorts views (90 days).

Other requirements: reside in a supported country/region, no active Community Guidelines strikes, 2-Step Verification on the Google Account, advanced feature access unlocked (phone-verify), one active AdSense for YouTube account linked. Account must be 30+ days old. Review typically ~30 days after applying.

Content quality note: YouTube tightened enforcement on "inauthentic content" in 2026 — AI-narrated stock-footage compilations, reused clips without real transformation/commentary, and mass-produced low-effort content are flagged and can block approval even if numeric thresholds are met.

## Instagram — general 2026 setup notes
- Switch to Professional account (Creator or Business) under Settings → Account type and tools.
- Creator = personal brand/content creators; Business = storefront/ads/formal partnerships.
- Professional Dashboard unlocks insights, monetization tools (Bonuses, Subscriptions, Gifts — eligibility varies by region/account standing).
- Creator Marketplace / Brand Collabs Manager requires opt-in and meeting eligibility criteria (follower/engagement based, not fixed — verify current numbers when relevant).
- Meta Verified is an optional paid tier for a badge + support, separate from monetization eligibility.

## Do not treat as final
Both platforms adjust thresholds multiple times a year. Treat the above as a "roughly where things stood in mid-2026" snapshot, and re-confirm live figures via search each time this skill runs.
